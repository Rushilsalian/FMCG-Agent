Assignment: AI-Powered Voice Agent for FMCG Retailer Engagement

Business scenario

Imagine a large FMCG company wants to use an AI-powered voice agent to interact with retailers, answer product-related queries, explain pricing and schemes, capture order enquiries, and record follow-up requirements.

The candidate is required to build a working prototype that simulates this experience.

 

Key requirements

Build a simple browser-based voice agent that allows a user to speak with an AI assistant and receive spoken responses. A text-based fallback should be available if voice features are not supported.

The AI assistant should answer retailer questions about products, prices, schemes, and availability using sample business data.

It should be able to capture product interest, requested quantities, and follow-up requirements.

At the end of the interaction, the application should generate a summary and save the conversation details and outcome.

The application should have a simple interface to start a conversation, interact with the AI agent, and review previous interactions.

 Tools and technology

Candidates are free to choose their technology stack and may use free AI tools, open-source models, libraries, and AI coding assistants.

Suggested options include Google AI Studio/Gemini API, Hugging Face, Ollama, and browser-based speech recognition and text-to-speech capabilities. Candidates may use equivalent alternatives.

 

Scope and assumptions

This is a prototype, not a production telecalling system. Candidates may use fictional FMCG products, sample prices, mock retailer records, and reasonable business assumptions.

 

Expected submission

Candidates should provide the source code, setup instructions, a working demonstration, and a brief explanation of their technical approach and assumptions. They should be prepared to demonstrate the application and explain their implementation during the interview.

The objective is to assess practical development skills, AI integration, problem-solving, code quality, and the ability to translate a business requirement into a working application.
